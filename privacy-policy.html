Privacy Policy

How Rade & Co Publishing collects, uses, and protects information.

Last updated: May 14, 2026. This page explains what information may be collected when visitors browse the website, submit forms, request bonuses, or sign up for updates.

At a glance
  • Information collected through forms, email signups, and bonus requests.
  • Use of analytics to understand page visits and site performance.
  • Use of Airtable and email delivery tools to manage content and communication.
  • How to request updates or removal of personal information.

Information collected

Rade & Co Publishing may collect information that visitors choose to submit, including names, email addresses, inquiry types, and messages through contact forms, bonus signups, and newsletter signup forms. The site may also collect limited usage information such as visited pages, general traffic patterns, and device-level analytics used to improve the website experience.

How information is used

Submitted information may be used to respond to inquiries, deliver requested bonus materials, send email updates, improve the website, and understand which pages and content are most helpful to readers. Subscriber information may also be used to notify readers about new books, blog posts, promotions, and related publishing updates.

Third-party services

The site may use trusted third-party tools to operate essential features, including Airtable for content and form data, Resend or similar email delivery services for contact and subscriber communication, and analytics tooling to understand traffic performance. These services may process information on behalf of Rade & Co Publishing only as needed to operate the site.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic cookies, local storage, and privacy-minded analytics to understand how visitors move through the website. This may include anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers used to measure total visits, page traffic, and overall site performance. Analytics are used to improve content and user experience rather than to build invasive advertising profiles.

Sharing and disclosure

Rade & Co Publishing does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to run the site, comply with legal obligations, protect the security of the website, or deliver requested content and communications.

Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain subscriber records, deliver requested resources, understand site performance, comply with legal obligations, and keep a working history of site operations. Data that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized.

Your choices and rights

Visitors may request access to, correction of, or deletion of submitted personal information where applicable. Visitors may also unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link provided in those messages or by contacting the brand directly.

Contact regarding privacy

Questions about this policy or requests related to personal information can be sent through the contact page or by email at radeandco@gmail.com.
