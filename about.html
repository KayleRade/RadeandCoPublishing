Meet Kate Rade

Rade & Co Publishing is rooted in story, family, and the desire to create books that feel both useful and lasting. Kate's work brings together practical tools, literary warmth, and a deeply personal love for meaningful reading experiences.

Personal story

A publishing journey shaped by heritage, motherhood, and timeless stories

Kate grew up with Eastern European roots and a deep love for classic storytelling. The fairy tales of Hans Christian Andersen, in particular, stayed with her for their beauty, emotional depth, and quiet sense of wonder.

Later, as she became a mother, those early influences returned in a new way. They shaped how she thought about the kinds of books she wanted to create: books that feel useful in everyday life, but also warm, meaningful, and worth keeping.

Today, through Rade & Co Publishing, Kate creates planners, journals, and family-friendly titles that reflect both sides of that vision. Some books help people organize and move through work with more clarity. Others invite reflection, connection, playful learning, and the kind of reading experiences that stay with you.

What stays at the center

Books can be practical and beautiful at the same time. They can help people manage real life while still making room for imagination, memory, and connection.

The mission behind Rade & Co Publishing

Rade & Co Publishing creates books that help people organize, reflect, connect, and enjoy meaningful reading experiences. The goal is to make titles that are thoughtfully designed, emotionally grounded, and easy to return to in everyday life.
