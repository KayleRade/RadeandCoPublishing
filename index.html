Planners, children's books, and journals by author Kate Rade

Rade & Co Publishing is an independent publishing brand founded by author Kate Rade. Every title is available on Amazon, and each one is designed to be as useful as it is beautiful.

  • Professional planners: the Travel Agent Planner keeps client details, bookings, and itineraries in one place, now in green and blue editions, and the Photography Planner organizes shoots, timelines, and creative prep.
  • Kids and family books: True or False Vol. 1 is a family game book, alongside classic-inspired storybooks such as The Little Mermaid, Thumbelina, and The Princess and the Pea.
  • Journals and wellness: the Coloring Mindfulness Journal pairs coloring with writing space for calmer routines.
  • Specialty titles: My Baking Secrets for recipes and baking notes, and The Ultimate Off-Road Adventures Handbook for documenting trails and trips.
